# Product

## Register
product

## Users
UK home students, 18+, mature or first in family to go to university, often on a phone. They apply to up to five courses per intake and want to know what to do next and where each application stands.

## Product Purpose
The educateU Applicant Hub: one profile reused across applications, a clear five-stage status for every application, a visible to-do list, offers with conditions, and main and insurance choice. Source: EducateU_Applicant_Hub_Requirements v1.0 (7 Oct 2026).

## Brand Personality
Calm confidence. Plain, warm, specific. A real person is near. British English, sentence case, no admissions jargon.

## Anti-references
UCAS jargon and abbreviations, long forms with no sense of progress, statuses that do not say what happens next, colour-only status, generic purple SaaS dashboards.

## Design Principles
1. One next step on every screen.
2. Enter once, reuse.
3. Plain status: what is happening, what happens next, when.
4. Balanced depth: summary first, detail one click away.
5. Mobile first and accessible. Status is never colour alone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA, responsive from 360px, every task completable on a phone, reduced motion respected.
