# Design

Source: EducateU brand guide (redesign/new-website). Ink carries the brand, mint is the single accent.

- Font: Manrope variable, hierarchy from weight and size. Display tracking -0.035em at most.
- Colour: Ink #013E5B, Mint #1EE9B5, Deep #012B40, Abyss #011E2D, Mist #F4F7F8, White, Slate #555F6D, Teal-on-light #0B7A5F (small text), Error #D23C3C, Notice lemon #FDF7C4 / olive #6B5A00.
- Shape: pills for controls, 28px cards, 16px fields, 12px chips. Ink-tinted low shadows only.
- Motion: cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1). Press scale .97 at 160ms, hover 300ms, panels 350-450ms. Transform and opacity only.
- Icons: Lucide, 2px stroke, round caps.
- Voice: plain, warm, British English, sentence case, label buttons with the action.
